# 🔤 Typography & Tracking Hierarchy (SF Pro & New York)

Complete specification for Apple's typographic scale, optical tracking (letter-spacing), and editorial serif pairing.

---

## 1. SF Pro Typography & Optical Tracking Scale

| Style | Font Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking (Letter Spacing) | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Large Title** | 34pt | 41pt | Bold (700) | `-0.8px` | Main header upon screen load |
| **Title 1** | 28pt | 34pt | Bold (700) | `-0.6px` | Section hero titles |
| **Title 2** | 22pt | 28pt | Bold (700) | `-0.4px` | Card headers, modal titles |
| **Title 3** | 20pt | 25pt | SemiBold (600) | `-0.2px` | Sub-headers, widget titles |
| **Headline** | 17pt | 22pt | SemiBold (600) | `-0.4px` | List item titles, button text |
| **Body** | 17pt | 22pt | Regular (400) | `-0.4px` | Long-form reading, journal entries |
| **Callout** | 16pt | 21pt | Regular (400) | `-0.3px` | Highlighted notices, input text |
| **Subheadline**| 15pt | 20pt | Regular (400) | `-0.2px` | List subtitles, metadata |
| **Footnote** | 13pt | 18pt | Regular (400) | `-0.1px` | Timestamps, hints |
| **Caption 1** | 12pt | 16pt | Medium (500) | `0px` | Tab labels, badge tags |
| **Caption 2** | 11pt | 13pt | SemiBold (600) | `0.1px` | Micro-counters, super-compact tags |

## 2. Pairing with New York (Editorial Serif)

For romantic, intimate, and reflective apps (such as Andrea App), pairing **New York** (or Cormorant / Playfair) for reflective titles with **SF Pro** for UI controls yields the hallmark Apple Journal aesthetic:
- **Reflections & Prompts**: Editorial Serif (32pt, italic, warm charcoal).
- **Controls, Badges & Dates**: SF Pro Grotesque (SemiBold, optical tracking).
